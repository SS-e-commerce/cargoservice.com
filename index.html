ABOUT CARGO SERVICE LLC

Since 2004, CARGO SERVICE LLC has been a pillar of reliability in the international air freight market. We have built a reputation for trust and professionalism that stands firm even in the most challenging times.

During the global disruptions of the COVID-19 pandemic, while many stopped, we continued to operate. We successfully organized the complex transportation of approximately 3,000 kg of catalytic converters from Armenia to the United States, proving our resilience and logistical capability.

Today, as the Official Representative of Ural Airlines in Armenia, we ensure uninterrupted cargo transportation between Russia and Armenia, despite international restrictions. We are dedicated to the safe and secure transport of your goods.

Furthermore, we specialize in the transportation of Dangerous Goods, managed by our certified professionals who ensure full compliance with international safety regulations.

FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Do you transport Dangerous Goods?

Yes, we hold the necessary certifications and have professional specialists to handle Dangerous Goods transportation safely and legally.

Can you ship cargo from Russia to Armenia now?

Absolutely. As the official representative of Ural Airlines, we ensure uninterrupted cargo flights between Moscow/Yekaterinburg and Yerevan despite restrictions.

How is the shipping cost calculated?

Cost depends on weight and volume. The volumetric weight is calculated as (Length x Width x Height) / 6000. You pay for the higher of the two weights.
